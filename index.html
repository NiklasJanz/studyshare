Democratizing Educational Resources

A peer-to-peer sharing ecosystem designed exclusively for college and university students. Exchange textbooks, electronics, and study tools without the need for a direct swap partner.

Why Choose StudyShare?

Asynchronous Exchange

Unlike traditional bartering, you do not need to find a direct match. The Points system provides immediate liquidity for your unwanted goods.

High-Trust Environment

Mandatory .edu verification eliminates the scams and safety risks associated with open platforms, guaranteeing a safe environment.

Frictionless Logistics

Integrated shipping labels mean students simply pack the item, attach the printed or QR-code label, and drop it off at campus mailrooms.

StudyShare Plus

A premium membership offering zero platform fees, discounted shipping rates, and free labels to provide recurring value for power users.

How It Works

1

Join

Sign up with your .edu email.

2

Give Away

List items you no longer need. When another student claims one, you earn Points.

3

Get What You Need

Use your Points to claim items from other students. You just pay a flat $5 shipping fee. No direct swap partner needed. If you don't have enough Points, you can buy more.

4

Ship

When someone claims your item, simply print the prepaid label and drop the item off at your campus mailroom. The buyer pays for shipping!

About StudyShare

Our Mission: To democratize access to educational resources and reduce the financial burden on students by building a trusted, sustainable, and entirely circular peer-to-peer sharing economy.

Founded by California college student Niklas Janz, StudyShare is initially focusing on the California market with plans to scale nationally, tapping into the 19 million college students across the United States to provide sustainable alternatives to expensive retail outlets.
